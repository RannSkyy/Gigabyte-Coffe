# ☕ Kopi GIGABYTE – Modern Coffee Shop Website

## 📌 Project Overview

**Kopi GIGABYTE** is a modern, elegant, and fully responsive coffee shop website designed to showcase the brand's identity, coffee products, and customer experience. It combines a premium coffee-inspired aesthetic with interactive features to create an engaging digital experience for coffee lovers.

The website features a warm espresso color palette, elegant typography, smooth animations, and intuitive navigation, providing visitors with a seamless browsing and ordering experience.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Header & Navigation
- Animated coffee cup logo and brand typography.
- Responsive navigation with active scroll indicators.
- Dynamic shopping cart counter.
- Mobile-friendly navigation drawer.
- Smooth scrolling and transition effects.

### 2. Hero Section
- Premium dark espresso background with digital grid patterns.
- Elegant coffee imagery and decorative elements.
- Bold typography with golden script accents.
- Call-to-action buttons for exploring and ordering coffee.

### 3. About Us
- Brand introduction and coffee philosophy.
- Interactive tabs for Micro Roasting, Direct Sourcing, and Brew Craft.
- Additional information modal.
- High-quality coffee bean photography.

### 4. Our Expertise
- Four interactive statistics cards.
- Highlights online stores, product varieties, experience, and customer satisfaction.
- Animated counters and detailed information modals.
- Responsive pagination indicators.

### 5. Coffee Menu
- Interactive category filters.
- Real-time menu search.
- Product cards with pricing and coffee imagery.
- Hot and cold drink options.
- Direct ordering buttons.
- Responsive pagination.

### 6. Coffee Gallery
- Aesthetic photo mosaic layout.
- Coffee photography featuring latte art, V60 brewing, roasted beans, and café atmosphere.
- Interactive image zoom.
- Lightbox with previous and next navigation.
- Barista descriptions for each image.

### 7. Customer Reviews
- Four stylish testimonial cards.
- Circular customer profile images.
- Star rating indicators.
- Interactive review submission modal.
- Dynamic customer review display.

### 8. Newsletter Subscription
- Clean and modern subscription form.
- Email validation.
- Automatic promotional coupon reward.
- Welcome discount code: `FIRSTCUP` ($5.00 off).

### 9. Contact & Branch Information
- Interactive contact form.
- Name, email, phone number, subject, and message fields.
- Form validation and submission feedback.
- Roastery address and business contact information.
- Daily operating hours.

### 10. Footer & Quick Navigation
- Concentric circular decorative patterns.
- Quick navigation links.
- Contact information and social media links.
- Instagram, Website, Pinterest, and Facebook icons.
- Copyright information (2026).
- Back-to-top button with circular scroll progress indicator.

## 🎨 Design & User Experience

The website uses a premium coffee-inspired visual identity with:

- **Color Palette:** Espresso black, warm brown, cream white, and golden accents.
- **Typography:** Bold modern fonts combined with elegant cursive lettering.
- **Layout:** Clean, balanced, and fully responsive sections.
- **Animations:** Smooth transitions, hover effects, and interactive elements.
- **Visuals:** High-quality coffee photography and decorative graphics.

## 📱 Responsive Design

Kopi GIGABYTE is designed to work seamlessly across different screen sizes:

- Desktop computers.
- Tablets.
- Mobile devices.

Navigation, content sections, product cards, and interactive components automatically adapt to different screen dimensions.

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Establish a strong and recognizable coffee shop brand identity.
- Present coffee products and services attractively.
- Provide an intuitive online ordering experience.
- Increase customer engagement through interactive features.
- Build a professional and accessible digital presence.

## 🏁 Conclusion

Kopi GIGABYTE combines modern web design, interactive functionality, and premium coffee aesthetics into a complete coffee shop website experience. It serves as a digital platform for brand promotion, product discovery, customer engagement, and online ordering.
